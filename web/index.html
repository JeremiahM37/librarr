Use your external Librarr URL plus /opds in reader apps.

Remote access: use HTTPS through your reverse proxy.

Integrations

Edit URLs and credentials without restarting the container. Saved values override the env vars in your compose file on next restart.

Anna's Archive

Your Anna's Archive account secret key (login key; also called a donator key in some tools) enables membership fast downloads via /dyn/api/fast_download.json. Without it, Librarr uses public LibGen mirrors. Fast downloads also require an active membership on that account.
